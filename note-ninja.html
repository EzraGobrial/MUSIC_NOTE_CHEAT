Three steps to get started

  1. Start the exercise

    Open the linked exercise and start a challenge. Click your Note Ninja bookmark to display the controls.

  2. Teach unfamiliar notes

    Answer new pictures yourself. The helper saves an example only after the correct-answer count increases.

  3. Let it recognize repeats

    With Auto enabled, matching pictures trigger their saved answers. Unrecognized pictures wait for you.

Your learning stays local

Saved examples stay in this browser on musictheory.net. Each person builds their own collection; nothing is uploaded by the helper.

Recognition compares exact pictures. Keep your browser zoom and window size consistent for the best chance of a match.

Using the controls

Use Auto to pause or resume automatic answers. Close removes the panel and stops automatic checking. Reset learning clears this browser’s saved examples.

Updating your bookmark

Replace your existing bookmark with the button above. Reload the exercise before running the new bookmark. Your saved Version 3 examples remain available.

Matching and performance

The helper checks once per screen refresh. Actual speed depends on your browser and computer. Identical full-resolution pictures are required for recognition.
